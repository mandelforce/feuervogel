Weak ones

    What a chosen design needs in the game

    • Flames: each mockup carries its own flame positions (flames in docs/player-art-v29.js), in the same form as the table in drawPlayer.
    • Hit box: I propose keeping the standard hit box for every ship except The Moth. The larger sprites grow in wings and tail, not in hull.
    • The Moth needs its own hit box, speed and a place in the ship list; that is game design, not art, and is not decided here.
    • The "A" variations are 37 px wide where today's sprites are 36: they are symmetric about a centre column, today's are one pixel lopsided.
    • The in-game views are composed from real ground and the game's flame rule. The ships were not flown in a level.
