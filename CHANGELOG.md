# Changelog

## 1.2.5 beta (Unreleased)
- The Enigma Run is now the Campaign. START flies its levels one after another: 1A, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B (level 5 leads to 6B). The old campaign lives on as Retro Run: a button at the end of the credits (from the title screen, Menu > Credits) opens its difficulty choice and starts it as it was. Retro Run never touches the leaderboard. Practice (under the Tutorial on the difficulty screen) now picks one of the six Campaign levels; practice runs are not ranked. Settings > Enigma Run is gone.
- The leaderboard shows one board, the Campaign. Gone: the Online / This device toggle and the Campaign / Endless toggle. A run that finishes level 6B opens the score dialog ("Campaign complete"). Offline, the board shows the last list it fetched (or this device's own scores if none) under "Offline: showing saved scores" with a Retry button; a score earned offline is sent automatically when the connection is back. Old saves, scores and boards are kept as they are.
- Gun speed is fixed at 90 % and point-blank fire is always on; both settings are hidden. The Enigma look slider is hidden (it stays off).
- Settings: "Last problem report" is now a button that opens the report on its own view (with Copy report and Back).
- Play-data reports now cover the Campaign and Retro Run (Retro Run is tagged `rt`); the version is 1.2.5. The replay tests pin the old gun speed (100 %) and point-blank off, so `tests/golden.json` stays valid.

- Enigma Run level 2, Sand Colossus last stand: the turret no longer sprays the dense double spiral. It fires salvos of three, one shot from the tip of each of its three barrels, while the turret swings across the player; every 4 to 6 seconds a burst of three salvos follows a 0.4 s warning: the turret locks on, three dotted aim lines run from the barrel tips, the tips glow and the charge sound plays. The Campaign keeps the spiral.
- The Arc (lightning weapon) is now a tether. It locks onto the nearest target in a narrower cone and keeps that lock while the target stays in reach; the longer it stays connected the harder it hits (up to 2x the base damage at level 1, 2.5x at level 4, reached after 45 to 60 frames). Losing the target, or switching to another, starts the ramp again. Base damage is lower, chain jumps now start at level 3 (they started at level 2) and hit for half the base damage with no ramp. Four looks: a 2 px bolt, a 2 px bolt with a muzzle flash, a 3 px bolt with a second strand, and a 3 px twin-strand bolt; every level thickens and whitens as it ramps and shows a ring on the target at full power. Boss damage per second with the bot is now level with the other weapons (the Arc was the lowest). Gameplay changed on purpose: `tests/golden.json` needs a refresh from CI for any scenario that flies the Arc.
- The Seekers (homing weapon) are weaker and have four distinct stages. The missiles no longer see the whole screen: each only locks onto what is in range (110 px at level 1 up to 200 px at level 4) and not behind it, turns more slowly at low levels, and flies a shorter distance. Level 1 fires one missile (alternating sides), level 2 a pair, level 3 a pair plus a centre missile, level 4 four missiles that burst on impact and hurt what is next to the target. Each level has its own bigger missile body and longer flame. The straight guns are now pink lasers that change per level (3 px soft-edged bolt, 5 px twin-edged bolt, 5 px white-core bolt with a tail, 7 px bolt with a pulse; level 4 does 20% more damage). Gameplay changed on purpose: `tests/golden.json` needs a refresh from CI for any scenario that flies Seekers.
- The Scatter (standard spread weapon) has a new look, drawing only (bullet counts, angles, hit box, damage, speed and fire rate are unchanged, and it uses no random numbers). Level 1 is a pair of rounded pellets. From level 2 the centre shot of the fan is a heavier dart and the outer darts are shorter and darker, so the fan reads as a volley with a leader; levels 3 and 4 add a short fading tail. It is kept light on purpose (no glow, glint or web, outlined like before) so the player's shots never hide enemy fire. The Talon drone's shots and the Seekers' straight pair keep the old pellet.
- The Lance (laser weapon) has a new look, drawing only (hit box, damage, speed and fire rate are unchanged, and it uses no random numbers). The bolt is built from nested shells, the inner ones shorter and further forward so the tip steps out like a telescope; each weapon level adds a shell (2 at level 1, 5 at level 4) and the bolt is 7 to 11 px wide (at least as wide as the old bolt with its outline), from a green outer shell to a pale core, with a tail that stretches in flight. At full power (level 4) bright rings also slide up the bolt. The Talon drone's shots stay plain grey.
- Enigma Run level 2 (Sea of Sand): new houses, a longer level and two drawing fixes (the Campaign and every other mode are unchanged). Every desert house is shaded like the Campaign's level 2 house (dithered tones, a stepped roof, lit edges); one village (the held one) is old, patched and half ruined, the others mostly kept up with a few worn houses, and the damage grows near the war areas. A new roadside hamlet lies in the quiet desert before the boss. The level is 72 chunks long instead of 56, about 2.5 screens more, with every stretch scaled up. The railway no longer runs through or beside the rock (at least 20 px clear), and the dry riverbed tapers into the sand instead of stopping in a straight cut. Details in docs/aged-desert.md.
- Enigma Run level 2, flare stacks: the flames of the refinery's flare, the oil valley's flare derrick and the offshore rig's flare boom are now animated: a flickering flame leaning with the wind, a faint glow (orange at night), and a thin trail of sooty smoke. They were painted flames before.
- Enigma Run level 2, chimney smoke: the refinery's chimney and the brickworks' two chimneys now give off animated black smoke, built from the burning-city smoke of level 6 (same animated layer) but darker, thinner at the foot and with no flames or glow. It rises and drifts with the wind, spreading as it thins.
- Enigma Run level 2, industry with chimneys, painted into the ground on clear open ground beside the road and away from the railway: a refinery at the mouth of the oil valley (two columns, three tanks, a pipe rack, a flare and a brick chimney, with long shadows), and a brickworks in the quiet desert before the boss (two corrugated kiln halls, two tall brick chimneys, a coal heap, a clay pit and a hut).
- Enigma Run level 2, oil field and offshore rig: the oil valley's derricks are redrawn as four-legged lattice towers with a crown block, a long shadow and a mud pit, painted straight into the ground (no pad); one derrick also has a flare stack burning off gas, joined to it by a pipe, with scorched ground. Off the port stands an offshore rig in the open sea: four legs in foam, a deck with a derrick, a helipad, a cabin and a flare boom. All of them keep clear of the railway, the roads and tracks, the ship lanes and the harbour's water; the track out to each derrick now runs 3 px further from it.
- Enigma Run (levels 2, 6, 6B): the Campaign's factory chimneys are no longer placed over a settlement plan's ground either (they stood in odd places, level 2 included). The plan's own stacks are unchanged.
- Enigma Run level 2: the tower standing in the sea beside the port was the Campaign's city church spire (and its city blocks), which was still being placed over the settlement plan's ground. Plan levels no longer get them; the station's own clock tower stands by its hall as before.
- Settlement generator, the station: the train shed is 7 px wider on the side away from the station hall, so the main line no longer runs right along its outer wall (9 px of room became 16).
- Enigma Run, the station's train shed has a see-through glass roof: a train inside shows through it, while the iron frame and ribs stay solid.
- Enigma Run, the formation leader has its own look: the Fw 190 with a red spinner and cowl, a red tail fin and white wing tips (the red and white ribbon behind it stays). The classic leader is unchanged outside Enigma Run.
- Enigma Run, new art for the units and a few new behaviours (the Campaign and every other mode are unchanged). The picked designs from the lineup tool (docs/names-and-behaviour.md) are baked into the page and shown only while an Enigma Run level is flown: the three tanks, the truck, armoured car, motorbike with sidecar, Kübelwagen and fuel truck, the five rail units, the S-boat, Z-class destroyer and U-boat, and the Fw 190, Bf 110 heavy fighter, He 111 bomber and Sower. Their hit boxes grow with the sprites. New or changed behaviour, also Enigma Run only: the Pfeil (Do 335, level 3 on) comes in pairs and fires forward, then backward after it has passed you; the heavy fighter fires its spread from both engines, twice from level 3; the bomber's four engines are four damage points (each one lost slows it and burns); the Lancer, a long-gun tank, joins convoys from level 2 (faster shell, slower turret); the motorbike is armed (its passenger fires a burst of two) and tougher; the truck's soldiers jump out when it is destroyed; the fuel truck explodes wider and sets the next one off; the staff car speeds up when it is hit or shots pass close.
- Two-player score boards. The stage results screen shows both players' scores in a strip under the title (1P left, 2P right, a crown beside the leader), counting up as the bonus is added; the TOTAL BONUS line shows each player's half. Stage bonuses are now split evenly between the two players (before, player 1 got all of it). The final CONGRATULATIONS screen lists TEAM SCORE, then 1P SCORE and 2P SCORE with a crown beside the leader, and the GAME OVER screen shows the team score, both players' scores and who leads (or TIE). Single-player screens are unchanged.
- PC: during play the menu (hamburger) and pause buttons in the top right corner are gone: Esc or P pauses and opens the menu (touch devices keep the buttons). In two-player games player 2's score now sits in that corner, mirroring player 1's score on the left, and a tiny crown marks whoever has the higher score. Player 2's weapon, Nova bombs and lives stay in the bottom right.
- Two-player: a player 2 who has run out of lives no longer stays out for the rest of the run. Three seconds after going out, it comes back by spending a spare life of player 1's, as soon as player 1 has two or more in reserve (player 1 always keeps one). A continue refills player 1's lives, so player 2 can rejoin after one.
- Two-player: player 2 now has the super laser too. Hold player 2's ship still to charge the ring at its nose, then move to fire it. It has its own charge, heat and overheat, breaks armoured targets like player 1's laser, and its kills score for player 2. Player 2's laser does not take part in the Black Fortress beam clash (only player 1's does).
- Enigma Run, the station can be destroyed: the train shed (and the station hall, where it still stands) is now a ground target (60 and 40 hit points). Destroyed, its roof falls in as a burning rubble mound, and a shed that is gone no longer covers its trains. A train inside the shed cannot be shot, as under a gallery, so shots at it hit the shed.
- Enigma Run, the station's train shed: trains no longer run over the glass roof. The roof is drawn again over the trains, so they roll in under it and come out the other side (as under the gorge's galleries).
- Enigma Run level 2, clear ways: the port's asphalt road now wears through to the dirt track in patches over 64 rows instead of ending at a hard line, and the valley's rock walls rise from the join instead of starting as a straight cliff edge, so the track no longer looks cut where the port meets the desert.
