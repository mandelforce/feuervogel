# Changelog

## 1.2.5 beta (Unreleased)
- Campaign level 1 and the Shadow Wing, after playtesting (build 1.2.5.12). The Shadow Wing is now its own encounter in every Campaign level: 2 s of calm, then the Wing, which has the screen to itself for about 15 s (no new air waves, ground units or pods); after that the level carries on around it if it is still flying. In level 1 it comes later, after the airbase island. Level 1 has no Drilling any more (it first appears in level 2), only one submarine (in the island chain, not on the open sea after the carrier), and the flagship sends one submarine at about half its health instead of two on a timer. Retro Run is unchanged. Gameplay change: `golden-enigma.json` is refreshed.
- Encounters, after playtesting (build 1.2.5.10; still only with the test link): Flak lane now places one gun on each side with a lane between them, or is called off if the ground doesn't allow it (the test link tries again a little later). Every encounter is one size bigger: The Anvil sends two formations at once and a dive, Flak lane a second formation and a strafing run of five, Bomber screen dives of five (and a second Bomber on Ace), the Sower Window a fourth tank convoy. Their bullet budgets were raised to match.
- Enigma Run, the Pfeil is a dart now (build 1.2.5.11). It comes in alone from one side of the screen, flies fast (2.9 px a frame, the hornet's 2.2) and mostly straight, and turns in wide circles of about 70 px. It may leave the screen on a circle and curves back in, and it never retreats: it keeps flying until it is shot down. On a straight leg that lines its nose up with the nearer ship (150 to 330 px away) it shoots: a small flare at the nose for 12 frames, then a burst of two along the nose (three on Hard or a later loop); it breaks away in a turn before it flies into a ship. From level 4 it sometimes has 2 wingmen (4 from level 5 or a later loop) in a V formation behind and beside it. The leader wears yellow markings; the wingmen fly and shoot one after another, never two at once. Shoot down the leader and the nearest wingman takes over (and the yellow); shoot down the whole formation for 2500 a member. The old hunting Pfeil and its Wingman Formation are kept as the Kolibri (`kolibri`, `KOLIBRI`, `kolibriTick`, `waveKolibri`, `waveKolibriWing`); nothing spawns it. New `tests/pfeil.mjs`.
- Encounter playtesting: `?enc=all` plays one encounter per level in a fixed tour (1A The Anvil, 2 Flak lane, 3 Bomber screen, 4 Sower Window, 5 The Anvil, 6B Flak lane), and with the test link a banner names each encounter as it starts, while it waits and when it ends. Normal play is unchanged.
- Encounter director, Sower Window (build 1.2.5.9; no change to play): the fourth encounter, for playtesting with `?enc=sowerwindow` in level 4. When the level's second Sower settles over the forest, three tank convoys come up the road below (two on Rookie). The level's first Sower stays on its own. No other encounter starts while a Sower or the Shadow Wing is up.
- Encounter director, Bomber screen (build 1.2.5.8; no change to play): a third encounter for playtesting with `?enc=bomberscreen`. A Bomber comes in while three-plane dives arrive at 1, 3 and 5 seconds, then a strafing run (Rookie: two dives, no run). No new ground units appear while it runs.
- Encounter director, Flak lane (build 1.2.5.7; no change to play): a second encounter for playtesting with `?enc=flaklane`. Two flak guns come first, a formation follows once they are on screen, then a strafing run from the side you are not on (not on Rookie). It only starts over land, and its guns use a level's planned gun sites where there are some.
- Encounter director, soft bullet budget (build 1.2.5.6; no change to play): while an encounter runs, fighters, the Heavy, the Bomber, flak, tanks, pop-up turrets and the Drilling ask before each whole volley and wait when the screen is full; nothing is deleted. The Sower's aerial mines never count (they are meant to fill the air), its falling bombs do. A volley fired over several frames reserves its bullets until its last one. Only reachable with `?enc=anvil` for now.
- Encounter director, groundwork (build 1.2.5.5; no change to play): the game can now play an authored encounter, The Anvil, but nothing in normal play starts one. New tests: `tests/encounter.mjs`, a Campaign replay baseline (`replay.mjs --enigma`) and a mid-encounter resume scenario.

Deer used to bolt straight across rivers and the sea, cows only kept to their paddock and sheep and horses only avoided rivers. An animal that ends up in the water anyway splashes, sinks and fades out with a trail of bubbles.

New look: an olive hull 16 x 34, a small wedge turret with a stereo rangefinder swept back in a V on both sides, and a long gun (33 px from the turret's pivot to the bore). The rangefinder's arms are dark olive under the turret and its lenses are glass, so the steel gun is the one bright spoke and its muzzle the one light. A lighter olive at dusk and night. Move off at the lock and it misses; hold still and it hits. It aims only once it is well on the screen. Its turret has its own health, like the Drilling's: many ordinary hits, an untouched one outlives a Nova, a full laser pops it; a popped turret ends the cycle at once and the tank drives on. Hull HP 14, turret 42, score 1500. New test: `tests/buechse.mjs`.

- Resume now carries on exactly where you stopped. While you fly, the game keeps a snapshot of everything that decides what happens next (your ship, every enemy and bullet, the pickups, craters, timers and the random numbers), twice a second and whenever you pause. If the phone closes the page while it is in the background (common after 20 minutes or more), the game offers Resume on the next start and puts you back at the same place: same score, enemies and bullets, then a three-second countdown. Before, you restarted the stage. Details:
  - The snapshot is only taken in a moment it can be written down exactly (not while a delayed effect such as a volley is pending, at the results screen, or at the continue prompt). If the page is left in such a moment, the last good snapshot is used, usually a fraction of a second older, at most a few seconds in the middle of a boss's destruction.
  - It needs the same build of the game and a screen of nearly the same height. After an update, or on a very different screen, a saved run still resumes the old way (the stage restarts with the lives, bombs and weapon you had).
  - The old save also became more reliable: it is refreshed every 5 seconds while you play (a page killed without a last event lost the run), it is no longer deleted when you come back to the page, saves from an earlier hidden build of the same version still resume, and a save is kept for 48 hours (was 6).
  - Under the hood: a few delayed callbacks (`warn`, stage clear banner, boss and wing death) are now named functions so they can be saved by name. No change to play (the replay test on the same screen size matches). New tests: `tests/resume.mjs` (a real reload) and `tests/resume-exact.mjs` (a restored run must play on exactly like the uninterrupted one, on all six Campaign levels, the old stages, bosses, two players and Endless).
